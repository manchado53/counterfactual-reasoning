the one big idea

Training vs what we do

🏋️ Training (already done)

Play millions of games, update the weights, get stronger. We never redo this — players are frozen.

📏 Our run (suitability)

Load a frozen player. Zero learning. Play pretend games to measure: "does the move decide the game here?"

TRAIN = play + learn.  US = play + measure.

We don't list boards — we collect the ones the player walks into

Connect 4 has ~4.5 trillion boards. We can't list them. So the frozen player plays the foe; each game traces one path down the tree, and we keep the boards it touches.

The grid: player skill × foe strength

One mcts-trained player, sampled at growing win rates, rolled against all three foes. Cells colored by SNR (green = clean signal, red = drowned). Click a cell to load it into the lab above.

5
what comes out

Each cell → one scorecard of 6 cheap metrics

SNR
is the fork louder than the noise?
Concentration
a few big forks to aim at?
DISTINCT-TD
does CCE rank moves unlike PER?
NEED
do high-stakes boards get visited?
Horizon-fit
rollout long enough to see the win?
GAIN-fidelity — n/a
no solver for C4 → no ground truth.

No oracle in C4, so these are descriptive — trusted only because the FrozenLake honesty check passed.

Recap: frozen player → play games → collect boards → for each board try all legal moves, finish N times → stakes = best − worst → crunch into 6 metrics. The foe's replies are the noise. Repeat per cell of the player×foe grid. No weights ever move.
